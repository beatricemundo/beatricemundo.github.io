Teaching

I teach French and Italian at Baruch College, CUNY. In my classes students practice in pairs and groups, working through interactive exercises that I design and build with Claude, Anthropic’s AI assistant, and checking their answers as they go.

Exercises built with Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Anthropic).

Interactive exercises

A first selection of the exercises I use in class. They run in the browser, with no sign-in, and are meant to be done in pairs or small groups.

Elementary French, open to everyone

An open French course I’m building for anyone who wants to learn the basics: no enrollment, no affiliation, no prior knowledge needed. It is at an early stage: for now it includes only the preliminary chapter, and more chapters, grammar explanations and practice activities will be added over time.
